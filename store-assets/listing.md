**App name:** Alco Store — Liquor Baku

Alco Store is the official app of a liquor store in Baku, Azerbaijan (Asif Maharramov St. 33A).

What you can do:
• Catalog — whisky, cognac, vodka, beer, tequila, liqueur and vermouth, with photos, volume and price
• Search — quickly find a product by name
• Cart — pick products, change quantities, add an optional note
• Order via WhatsApp — your cart is sent as a ready-made message to the store's WhatsApp, and the operator confirms the order with you
• Notifications — new arrivals and store news (optional, can be turned off any time)

No account or registration is required, and no payments are made in the app.

• Delivery within Baku only.
